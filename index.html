Ask us anything
How do I know you're legit?

Loom Wave International Pte. Ltd. is incorporated in Singapore, UEN 202551417C. You can look it up yourself on uen.gov.sg. Every order is covered by a signed agreement before production starts, and you can video call us anytime.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. You can order a sample first without committing to bulk. You check the fit, material, workmanship and decoration, and bulk production only begins once you have approved it.

How long does it take?

Your quotation arrives within 2 working days of receiving your design. Sampling usually takes 13 to 15 working days. Bulk production normally takes 14 working days after you approve the sample. Your quotation confirms the schedule.

Do you ship to my country?

Very likely. We have served 19+ countries, including the Netherlands, the UK, France, Greece, the USA, Canada, Japan and Malaysia. We ship from Bangladesh to your destination, confirm the delivery terms in your quotation, and send tracking after dispatch.

Can you help if I only have an idea?

Yes. You don't need a tech pack. A photo, a sketch or a reference and a conversation are enough to get started, and the same team guides you from first message to delivery.
